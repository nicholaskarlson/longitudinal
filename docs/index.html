Check the teaching calculations

In the unoffered group, 256 people have scorable stress items at all four waves. Their mean paired baseline-to-month-12 change is +0.01484375 on the stated score scale. Among all 1,219 baseline respondents, the registry records 173 withdrawals, 43 transfers, and 11 consent-withdrawal censorings. Exact-time competing-risk accounting gives a 12-month withdrawal cumulative incidence of about 0.14254.

See the repository README and code for the scoring and event-time rules. The values are descriptions of selected fictional records. The paired result does not adjust for missingness or sampling. The event calculation treats transfer as a competing outcome and consent withdrawal as censoring.

Claim ceiling: matching checksums establish data identity, and matching routes establish agreement for the specified arithmetic. Neither establishes causal identification, validity of measurement, independent censoring, or real-world generalizability.
